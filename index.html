The Opportunity

A flagship Dolby Atmos mixing and post facility in Hyderabad, built by the Synccinema team. Phase 1 is ₹6 Cr, with ₹4.5 Cr already committed. Round 1 raises the remaining ₹1.5 Cr. The facility is built in three phases to a total of ₹15 Cr.

The Three Phases

Yantra is a ₹15 Cr facility built in three phases. Only Phase 1 is active and open for investment right now — Phases 2 and 3 follow later, funded from the facility’s own cashflow and future rounds once Phase 1 is operational.

Our History

Synccinema was founded in 2014 in Chennai by sound designers Sachin Sudhakaran and Hariharan Muralidharan. In under a decade it has grown into one of India’s most sought-after audio post-production teams — with credits on 200+ feature films across Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, Malayalam and Kannada, and a National Film Award to its name. Yantra is the team’s next chapter: a flagship facility to bring that craft under one roof.
